## The idea

Dinosaurdle is a turn-based twist on the New York Times' Wordle: guess the five-letter word before the dinosaur catches up with you.

## What I built

This was my introduction to web development. Building it taught me the fundamentals of JavaScript, HTML and CSS, along with practical experience of APIs and client-server communication. I followed WCAG guidelines to keep the game accessible.
