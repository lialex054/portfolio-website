## The problem

Disposable nappies are a massive source of waste in the UK. The issue isn't one product's material; it's a whole system of buying, using and throwing away.

## The approach

We used the STEEP framework to look at the problem across social, technological, economic, environmental and political factors. That pointed us away from designing a better product on its own and towards a better business model: a product-service system.

## What we designed

BloomBums is a closed-loop subscription. Families receive fully recyclable liners, and used ones are collected and recycled rather than sent to landfill. We worked through the service and the market, and visualised the product and packaging in Blender and Fusion 360.
