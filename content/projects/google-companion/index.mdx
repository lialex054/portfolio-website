## The problem

Young adults with ADHD often struggle to stay focused, and just as often struggle to transition smoothly out of hyperfocus once they're in it. Through user and expert interviews, we mapped the productivity pain points they ran into day to day.

<Figure src="/work/google-companion/concept-development.webp" alt="Concept development board with storyboard and user validation" caption="Concept development: iterative design, user validation and storyboard." />

## What we built

We designed a subtle desktop companion that guides each user through their focus journey, paired with a phone app. The experience is personalised rather than one-size-fits-all.

<Figure src="/work/google-companion/at-desk.webp" alt="Google Companion in use at a desk" />

## How we got there

Continuous iteration shaped the product: we created over 20 component variations to get the experience right, testing each with users.

<Figure src="/work/google-companion/prototyping-testing.webp" alt="Physical prototyping and testing board" caption="Physical prototyping and testing." />

## The result

The experience proved highly impactful: user testing showed a 60% increase in study time.
