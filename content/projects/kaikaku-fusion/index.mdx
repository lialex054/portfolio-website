## The brief

KAIKAKU, a startup, hired me to create a trailer for their new flagship product, Fusion. The film needed to communicate their vision and launch the product with impact.

## What I did

I owned the trailer end to end: storyboarding, creating the 3D assets and editing the final cut. I worked in Blender and Adobe Premiere Pro, collaborating closely with the KAIKAKU team so the film matched their brand identity.
