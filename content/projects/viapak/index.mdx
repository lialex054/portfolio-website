## The problem

Medicine packaging is hard to open for older adults and people with arthritis. User research confirmed it: the people we spoke to struggled with existing designs and felt they were losing their independence over something as routine as taking their medication.

## How we worked

We ran the project as a discovery loop rather than a single design pass. Every prototype went back to our target users, and what we learned shaped the next one.

Prototype testing showed high user satisfaction and a renewed sense of autonomy, validating the human-centred process behind the final design.
