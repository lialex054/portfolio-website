## The problem

General-purpose navigation apps are built for cars and pedestrians, not for the unpredictable nature of cycling. We saw a gap in the market for a tool made specifically for cyclists.

Our user research made the gap concrete: cyclists' biggest worries were **poor road conditions** and **inattentive drivers**, and none of the apps they used helped with either.

## What we built

We designed Cyclogic, a navigation app built around a customer-driven database of routes with real-time updates from other riders. The goal was to give cyclists crucial information in a form they could take in at a glance while riding.

User testing gave overwhelmingly positive feedback: riders found the information crucial and easy to digest. We wrote up the market opportunity and business case in a full report.
